City Metro Trip-Planner is a mobile-first single-page web application that lets riders plan journeys across rail and bus lines, view service alerts, browse an interactive transit map, and manage their transit account — all from one fast, accessible interface.

The app ships with a fully functional demonstration mode so you can explore every feature immediately. When you're ready to go live, swap in your own OAuth 2.0 provider and trip-data backend without touching the UI code.
